Power Meter Monitoring — Modbus RTU

Wireless acquisition of electrical measurements from power meters using Modbus RTU and an ESP32-based interface.

Technology & scope

  • Modbus RTU
  • RS485
  • ESP32
  • Wireless communication
  • Dashboard monitoring

Architecture

Power Meter → RS485/Modbus RTU → ESP32 → Wireless/API → Database → Dashboard

Engineering outcome

The solution turns electrical measurements into centralized, historical data for monitoring and analysis.
